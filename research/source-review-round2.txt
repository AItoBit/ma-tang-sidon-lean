CANDIDATE ROUTE FOR 4.6, NOT A PROOF
Remove a vertex of degree>=3: one transversal point costs at most four units
of the n+m potential. Maximum-degree<=2 hypergraphs dualize to a subcubic
multigraph; a matching bound nu>=edges/4 would finish that case. Shannon's
four-edge-colorability would supply that matching bound, but neither the
full reduction nor the matching estimate is proved here. No such claim is
permitted as an axiom or an implicit dependency.
